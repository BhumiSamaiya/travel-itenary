# Demo video storyboard (about 50 seconds)

1. **0–7s — Welcome:** Show the Wayfarer dashboard and tagline, “Make room for the unexpected.”
2. **7–16s — Build your trip:** Enter destination, dates, budget, travelers, travel vibe, and interests.
3. **16–27s — Generate:** Click “Generate my itinerary”; show the day-by-day schedule and activity cards.
4. **27–36s — Personalize:** Change the travel vibe or interests and regenerate.
5. **36–44s — Save:** Click “Save this trip” and show it under Saved trips.
6. **44–50s — Wrap-up:** Show the estimated budget and note that prices and recommendations are illustrative.

Suggested narration: “Wayfarer makes trip planning feel simple. Add your destination, dates, budget, and travel style. Generate a structured day-by-day plan, adjust it to match your interests, and save the trip for later. The prototype demonstrates a React interface backed by a FastAPI itinerary endpoint, with an offline demo generator and optional Supabase integration.”

Note: The included MP4 is a visual storyboard preview, not a live screen recording of the application.
